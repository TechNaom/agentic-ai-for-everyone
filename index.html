Why This Course Is Different

The Loop, Not One Model Call

`ai-engineering-for-everyone` teaches prompting and fixed pipelines. This course teaches what happens once the model itself decides, iteratively, what to do next -- and how to keep that loop correct, safe, and bounded.

Architecture, Not One Domain or One Protocol

`ai-coding-agents-for-everyone` builds a coding-specific agent; `mcp-for-everyone` teaches a tool-transport protocol. This course teaches the general loop, planning, memory, and multi-agent architecture underneath any domain or protocol.

Reliability and Cost as Systems Problems

An agent that can act on its own needs guardrails, observability, and cost control as first-class design concerns -- not an afterthought bolted on once something breaks in production.
