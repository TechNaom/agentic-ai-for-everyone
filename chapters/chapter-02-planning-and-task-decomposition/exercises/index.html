Chapter 2 Exercises ~40 min, hands-on

Exercises: Planning and Task Decomposition

These exercises use a second scenario, deliberately different from the lesson's Alderleaf Research Group/ScoutBot hook: Pinehurst Realty Group, a fictional residential brokerage. Its agent, ListBot, handles two kinds of task: listing a new property (a fixed, up-front plan) and diagnosing why a showing fell through (an emergent, one-step-at-a-time investigation). Applying this chapter's concepts to a fresh scenario is the point.

Exercise standard Eight tasks total, worth 16 points. Five are marked production-gear — writing a real fixed plan, fixing a real wrong-argument bug, implementing a real emergent decision function, diagnosing a real wrong-plan failure trace, and implementing a real re-plan guard, not just concept recall.

Tasks

  1. Map facts to planning concepts — assign fixed plan, emergent plan, re-planning, wrong-plan failure, or planning guard to five ListBot facts.
  2. Dependency reasoning — does pre-committing a step before an earlier result is known make a wrong-plan failure more or less likely?
  3. (Production-gear) Fixed plan — write the ordered 3-step listing plan as real data.
  4. (Production-gear) Normalize fix — tolerate a realistic wrong-argument variation.
  5. (Production-gear) Emergent decision function — the right follow-up check depends on what the showing log says.
  6. (Production-gear) Failure diagnosis — correctly name a wrong-plan failure.
  7. (Production-gear) Re-plan guard — bound retries with max_replans.
  8. Completeness check — confirm all five planning concepts were actually used.

Full instructions and the scoring rubric are in README.md alongside starter.py (fill in the TODOs) and solution.py (the reference, scores 16/16).
