Chapter 5 Exercises ~40 min, hands-on

Exercises: Reflection and Self-Correction

These exercises use a second scenario, deliberately different from the lesson's Briarcliff Bike Rentals/BikeBot hook: Fenwick Home Repair Co-op, a fictional handyman referral service. Its estimate agent, RepairBot, drafts a price quote for a repair job and must flag jobs that need a licensed technician instead of quoting them as ordinary handyman work. Applying this chapter's concepts to a fresh scenario is the point.

Exercise standard Eight tasks total, worth 18 points. Five are marked production-gear — a real grounded price verifier, a real licensed-referral policy check, a real reflect_and_revise composition, diagnosing a real "retry is not reflection" trace, and a real bounded reflect loop, not just concept recall.

Tasks

  1. Map facts to reflection concepts — assign first-pass draft, reflection step, grounded verification criteria, revise-the-answer correction, or iteration bound to five RepairBot facts.
  2. Dependency reasoning — does reflecting on every request regardless of stakes make unnecessary cost more or less likely?
  3. (Production-gear) Grounded price verifier — check a stated price against an independently recomputed one.
  4. (Production-gear) Licensed-referral policy check — detect a job that needs a licensed technician, and whether a draft says so.
  5. (Production-gear) reflect_and_revise composition — combine both checks, revising only when actually wrong.
  6. (Production-gear) Failure diagnosis — correctly name "retry is not reflection" from a trace.
  7. (Production-gear) Bounded reflect loop — stop after a fixed number of attempts and escalate to a human.
  8. Completeness check — confirm all five reflection concepts were actually used.

Full instructions and the scoring rubric are in README.md alongside starter.py (fill in the TODOs) and solution.py (the reference, scores 18/18).
