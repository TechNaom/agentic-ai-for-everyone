Chapter 4 Exercises ~40 min, hands-on

Exercises: Memory and State

These exercises use a second scenario, deliberately different from the lesson's Larkspur Fitness Studio/CoachBot hook: Driftwood Legal Clinic, a fictional legal-aid clinic. Its intake agent, IntakeBot, needs to remember accessibility accommodations and scheduling preferences a client states in ONE visit so a receptionist booking a follow-up hearing weeks later automatically gets it right. Applying this chapter's concepts to a fresh scenario is the point.

Exercise standard Eight tasks total, worth 16 points. Five are marked production-gear — a real promote-worthy classifier, a real read-modify-write add function, a real working-context merge, diagnosing a real merge-bug trace, and a real promote-before-prune function, not just concept recall.

Tasks

  1. Map facts to memory concepts — assign short-term working memory, long-term persisted memory, token-budget pruning, promote-worthy fact detection, or read-modify-write merge bug to five IntakeBot facts.
  2. Dependency reasoning — does pruning working memory before a fact is persisted make permanently losing it more or less likely?
  3. (Production-gear) Promote-worthy classifier — distinguish an accommodation or preference from routine chat.
  4. (Production-gear) Read-modify-write add function — fix the blind-overwrite bug, don't repeat it.
  5. (Production-gear) Merge into a fresh working context — retrieve persisted facts and inject them correctly.
  6. (Production-gear) Failure diagnosis — correctly name a read-modify-write merge bug from a trace.
  7. (Production-gear) Promote-before-prune — ensure a fact survives even after its working-memory turns are gone.
  8. Completeness check — confirm all five memory concepts were actually used.

Full instructions and the scoring rubric are in README.md alongside starter.py (fill in the TODOs) and solution.py (the reference, scores 16/16).
