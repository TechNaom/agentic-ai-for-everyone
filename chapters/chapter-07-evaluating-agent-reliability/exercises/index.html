Exercises: Evaluating Agent Reliability

These exercises use a second scenario, deliberately different from the lesson's Greywick Dispatch/FactScout hook: Larkmoor Archive Service, a fictional historical-records office. Its research agent, RecordScout, can search a property registry, check a record's authenticity, cross-reference a deed, generate a research report, or escalate to a human archivist. Applying this chapter's eval concepts to a fresh scenario is the point.

Exercise standard Seven tasks total, worth 19 points. Five are marked production-gear — a real trajectory_correctness check, a real task_success check, a real pass_at_k estimator, a real eval-gap diagnosis function, and a real cost_per_success calculator, not just concept recall.

Tasks

  1. Map facts to eval concepts — assign task success, trajectory correctness, pass@k, cost-per-success, or multi-turn drift to five RecordScout facts.
  2. Dependency reasoning — does a task-success check that only verifies tool presence catch a content error inside a tool's own output?
  3. (Production-gear) trajectory_correctness — exact-order comparison against a ground-truth trajectory.
  4. (Production-gear) task_success — a forgiving, order-independent tool-presence check.
  5. (Production-gear) pass_at_k — the unbiased pass@k estimator.
  6. (Production-gear) diagnose_eval_gap — classify an eval setup's biggest blind spot.
  7. (Production-gear) cost_per_success — cost divided by successes, not total runs.

Full instructions and the scoring rubric are in README.md alongside starter.py (fill in the TODOs) and solution.py (the reference, scores 19/19).
